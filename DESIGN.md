# Design Brief

## Direction

Convergence Lab is a restrained research workstation. It must communicate
evidence quality, uncertainty, failure, and risk before it communicates
opportunity.

## Interface Rules

- Dark, high-contrast surfaces with visible focus treatment.
- Research-only, qualified, and rejected states are always explicit.
- Fixture data is never visually confused with imported market data.
- Tables use semantic headers and remain horizontally scrollable.
- Every form control has an accessible label.
- No gradients, decorative charts, or confidence theater.
- Responsive layouts must remain usable at mobile widths.

## Signature Detail

The qualification pipeline is always visible: import, experiment, reject,
simulate, then forward-test.
